cal-sync privacy policy

cal-sync is a personal tool used only by its owner, Taj Haslani, to copy events between his own calendars.

  • What it accesses: calendar events (title, time, description, location) in the owner's own calendars.
  • How it is used: only to create, update and delete matching copies of those events in the owner's other calendars. Attendees are never copied and no invitations are sent.
  • Where data goes: the tool runs on the owner's private server. Calendar data is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train models.
  • Storage: the server keeps only event IDs and sync bookkeeping, plus OAuth tokens in a file readable only by the owner.
  • Revoking access: remove "cal-sync" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
